My config folder is in ~/.config/, where my skhd, nvim, tmux and karabiner config live. Do keep in mind that my tmux config gobbles up things like C-h, C-j, C-k, C-k and skhd gobbles up loads of alt keybindings such as alt- hjklnvF etc, so when designing something related to keybindings keep that in mind. I love to rice my setup with yabai but am thinking of moving to arch + hyprland, but not possible for the time being. My zsh config is in ~/.zshrc , and i have aliases for ls -> eza, cd -> zoxide and gst -> git status. I use lazygit as well. I use nvim org mode, and my org files are in ~/org/ . I am trying to make a little blog of my hobbies in /blog/ using hugo.
